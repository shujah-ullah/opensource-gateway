# Workflow Automation

The engines and template libraries that connect everything else — triggers, APIs, AI steps and business logic in one flow.

### [n8n-io/n8n](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n) — ⭐ ~212k
n8n is the fair-code workflow automation platform with native AI capabilities: a visual builder plus custom code, 400+ integrations, self-hosted or cloud. The automation backbone for most agencies.
Licence: fair-code (Sustainable Use Licence) — self-hosting for your own business is fine; embedding it in a product you sell is restricted.

### [Zie619/n8n-workflows](https://github.com/Zie619/n8n-workflows) — ⭐ ~57k
A giant collection of n8n workflows gathered from everywhere they could be found, including the official site. The fastest way to see how a workflow you need is usually built.

### [enescingoz/awesome-n8n-templates](https://github.com/enescingoz/awesome-n8n-templates) — ⭐ ~27k
280+ free, ready-to-use n8n templates for Gmail, Telegram, Slack, WhatsApp, Google Drive, Notion, OpenAI and more — AI agents, RAG chatbots, email and social automation, document processing.

### [czlonkowski/n8n-mcp](https://github.com/czlonkowski/n8n-mcp) — ⭐ ~23k
An MCP server that lets AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor build, debug, validate and test n8n workflows directly on your instance.

### [n8n-io/self-hosted-ai-starter-kit](https://github.com/n8n-io/self-hosted-ai-starter-kit) — ⭐ ~15k
n8n's own template for standing up a local, self-hosted AI environment quickly — a good starting point for private AI workflows.
