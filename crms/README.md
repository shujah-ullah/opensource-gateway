# CRMs

Open source customer relationship management — the system of record for your customers, pipelines and follow-ups, without a per-seat SaaS bill.

### [twentyhq/twenty](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty) — ⭐ ~61k
Twenty is the open alternative to Salesforce, designed for AI. Modern, customisable CRM with objects, views and automations; self-host it for free.
