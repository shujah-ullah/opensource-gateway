# Customer Communication

Self-hosted inboxes and live chat — every customer conversation from your website, email and social channels in one place.

### [chatwoot/chatwoot](https://github.com/chatwoot/chatwoot) — ⭐ ~40k
Chatwoot is an open source live-chat and omnichannel support desk — an alternative to Intercom and Zendesk. Website chat, email, WhatsApp and social inboxes in one self-hosted dashboard.
